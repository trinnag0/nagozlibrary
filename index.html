How The Desk Works

Two Publishing Tracks, One Goal

Every submission receives light-touch editorial polish with your final sign-off—never rewriting your voice. Choose how your piece enters the world:

Track 01 · Fast-Track 7–14 Days

The Public Archive

Ideal for ready-to-read poetry, personal reflections, and flash fiction. We publish your piece directly on NagozLibrary with a permanent URL and package your best lines into Instagram carousels and voiceover Reels.

  • ✓ Permanent resume-ready author credit
  • ✓ Co-authored Instagram & LinkedIn feature
  • ✓ You retain 100% copyright
Track 02 · Agent Desk Private Hold

The External Pitch Desk

Built for standout long-form essays, campus journalism, and literary fiction. We hold your draft privately—protecting your First Publication Rights—while helping you refine and pitch it to established external journals.

  • ✓ Protects First Publication Rights
  • ✓ 1-on-1 editorial polish & pitch email drafting
  • ✓ Matched to open calls in India & globally

Librarian Live CMS (Google Sheets Setup)

Scale to hundreds of student submissions without touching HTML again. Connect a free Google Sheet so any row marked Published appears automatically on The Shelf.

Step 1: Paste these exact headers in Row 1 (A1:L1)
title author affiliation school genre featured date readTime bio excerpt content status
  1. In Google Sheets, go to File → Share → Publish to web.
  2. Select your sheet tab, change Web page to Comma-separated values (.csv), click Publish, and copy the URL.
  3. Open index.html on GitHub, paste that URL into const SHEET_CSV_URL = "..." at the top of the script, and commit.
  4. To publish future poems, simply add a row in Google Sheets and set status to Published.

Install NagozLibrary

Add NagozLibrary directly to your phone or desktop home screen for instant offline-ready reading:

iPhone / iPad (Safari): Tap the Share icon at the bottom of your browser, scroll down, and tap Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): Tap the ⋮ Menu in the top-right corner and select Install app or Add to Home screen.

Desktop (Chrome / Edge): Click the Install icon inside the right side of the address bar.
