How Your Student Librarian Works

Two Tracks. Total Rights Protection.

Many literary journals reject pieces that have already appeared online. That’s why NagozLibrary lets you choose how your draft is handled before a single word goes public.

Track 01 • Fast-Track (7–14 Days)

The Public Archive

Designed for poetry, campus reflections, and short prose ready to be read right now. We lightly polish grammar and formatting, publish your work on NagozLibrary with a permanent Author Page, and transform your best lines into visual Instagram carousels and voiceover Reels.

  • ✓ Light-Touch Editing: Zero voice rewriting; author sign-off required before publishing.
  • ✓ Social Media Amplification: Custom serif quote cards & Instagram Collab posts.
  • ✓ Portfolio Ready: Permanent URL to link on LinkedIn, resumes, or grad applications.

Track 02 • Literary Scout

The External Pitch Desk

Designed for ambitious personal essays, investigative campus journalism, and polished fiction. We keep your manuscript strictly unpublished to protect your First Publication Rights while helping you refine it and pitch it to established literary magazines.

  • ✓ First Rights Protected: Kept private in our editorial vault until placed externally.
  • ✓ Pitch & Cover Letter Prep: 1-on-1 structural feedback and editor pitch drafting.
  • ✓ Curated Journal Matching: Matched to open calls across Indian & international journals.

Editorial Charter

The NagozLibrary Manifesto

Most student writers write in isolation—filling hostel notebooks and phone Notes apps without ever seeing their words formatted, edited, or read beyond a close friend group. NagozLibrary bridges that gap. Acting as your Student Librarian, we curate, lightly edit, archive, and amplify student voices from KIIT University and campuses worldwide.

Word Count Guidelines

  • • Poetry: Up to 3 poems (max 60 lines each)
  • • Personal Essays: 600 – 1,800 words
  • • Short Fiction: 300 – 2,000 words
  • • Cultural Commentary: 500 – 1,500 words

Light-Touch Edit Rule

We polish punctuation, line breaks, and clarity—never your distinct voice. Every edit is shared via Google Doc comments for your explicit approval prior to publication.

Zero Fees & Full Copyright

NagozLibrary never charges submission or reading fees. Writers retain 100% copyright and may republish their pieces in print anthologies, personal books, or external journals at any time.
